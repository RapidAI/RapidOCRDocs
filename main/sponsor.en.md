## Sponsor RapidOCR

RapidOCR is free and open source, and we intend to continue providing free technical support. As the project grows, however, ongoing maintenance and development require financial support.

Maintaining this ecosystem and developing new features is a substantial effort made possible by the generosity of our sponsors.

### Sponsor benefits

1. Exposure in the [Chinese and English README files](https://github.com/RapidAI/RapidOCR/tree/main#sponsors) and the official documentation.
2. Exposure in the [Chinese and English README files on Gitee](https://gitee.com/RapidAI/RapidOCR#sponsors).

### Corporate sponsorship

Sponsoring RapidOCR provides visibility among developers worldwide through our website and GitHub README. Supporting open source can also strengthen your brand among developer communities.

If RapidOCR supports a revenue-generating product, sponsoring its development helps keep a critical dependency healthy and actively maintained.

For developer-focused products, sponsorship can deliver relevant traffic, build awareness and improve conversion because our visitors are primarily developers.

### Individual sponsorship

If RapidOCR has improved your productivity, consider making a donation as a token of appreciation, perhaps the equivalent of an occasional coffee.

For donations above CNY 10, include `RapidOCR + GitHub ID / Gitee profile / blog URL` in the payment note. We will add you to the supporter list below.

#### Alipay or WeChat
